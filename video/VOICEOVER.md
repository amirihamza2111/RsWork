# RsWork promo (3 minutes) — voiceover script

About 380 words over 3:00, a calm and confident pace (about 2.3 words per second).
Each block starts when its scene starts. Record it, or paste it into a text-to-speech
tool (CapCut's TTS works), save as `video/out/voiceover.wav`, and re-run `video/render.ps1`:
the music automatically lowers under the voice.

| Time | Scene | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 – 0:09 | Intro | This is RsWork. Scan, order, serve — and grow. |
| 0:09 – 0:26 | The problem | Every busy restaurant knows it. Guests wait for a menu, a waiter, then the bill. Paper tickets get lost between the floor and the kitchen. And at closing time, you still don't know what really sold — or what tomorrow holds. |
| 0:26 – 0:38 | Meet RsWork | Meet RsWork: one platform that connects the table, the kitchen, the waiters, the cash register and the owner. And every screen updates in real time. |
| 0:38 – 1:04 | Guests | For your guests, it starts with a QR code on the table. No app to install. They browse your menu in French, English or Arabic, get suggestions that go well with their order, and send it straight to the kitchen. Then they follow it live — received, preparing, ready, served — pay by card or cash, and rate their meal in one tap. |
| 1:04 – 1:24 | Kitchen | In the kitchen, orders appear instantly on a live board. Every ticket has a timer, cooks claim their dishes, and each status change flows straight back to the guest and the waiters. No paper. No shouting. |
| 1:24 – 1:38 | Waiters | On the floor, waiters are alerted the moment a dish is ready or a table asks for the bill. Tables are shared fairly between them, and claiming an order means nobody serves it twice. |
| 1:38 – 1:50 | Cashier | At the register, every bill is settled in one tap — cash or card, exactly once — with a printable receipt. And the table is freed for the next guests. |
| 1:50 – 2:18 | Owner | For the owner, the whole restaurant fits on one screen. Live revenue, orders and best sellers. Update dishes, photos and prices in seconds. Create tables and print their QR codes. Add staff with the right role — and read every guest review. |
| 2:18 – 2:38 | AI & forecast | And RsWork looks ahead. It learns from your own sales to forecast the next seven days of orders and revenue, picking the most accurate model automatically. Then it writes a clear business report in plain language, ready to export as a PDF. |
| 2:38 – 2:50 | Under the hood | Under the hood: every restaurant's data stays private, card payments run on Stripe, and the platform is load-tested and security-tested. Nothing to install — it runs in any browser. |
| 2:50 – 3:00 | Call to action | RsWork. Turn every table into a faster, smarter service. Request your demo today. |
